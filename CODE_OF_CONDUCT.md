# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

TraceReplay aims to provide a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in the project.

We are committed to making participation free from harassment and discrimination, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Explaining disagreements without personal attacks.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and technical decisions.
- Helping keep discussions clear and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, intimidation, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Please include relevant context and links where possible.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take appropriate action. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently.
